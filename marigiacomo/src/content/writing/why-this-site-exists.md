I have tried to build this site four or five times and never finished. Every attempt died at
the same point: I got something online, then wanted to change one line, and could not remember
how any of it worked.

So this version is deliberately boring. Plain text files, no database, no admin panel. Adding a
post means writing a file. If I ever open this in three years and remember nothing, it will
still make sense.

The plan is to write about what I am reading and building. Right now that means the
semiconductor industry, large language models, and the odd experience of rebuilding software I
once paid a team to make. I ran a company for four years and closed it, so some of it will be
about that too.

No schedule. If it turns out I have nothing to say for a month, the last post can sit here
looking slightly stale, and that is fine.
