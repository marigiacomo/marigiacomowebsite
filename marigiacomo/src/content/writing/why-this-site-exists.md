I have tried to build this site four or five times and never finished. My lack of competencies in coding and my reluctance to learn stopped me every time. I wanted something fancy, eye-catching, stunning, but every time I tried I failed to create something that could deliver my message properly.

So this version is deliberately boring. Plain text files, no database, no admin panel. Adding a
post means writing a file. If I ever open this in three years and remember nothing, it will
still make sense. 

I want the focus to be on content.

The plan is to write about what I am reading and building. Right now that means the 
semiconductor industry, large language models, and the odd experience of rebuilding software I
once paid a team to make. I ran a company for four years and closed it, so some of it will be
about that too. I'd also like to write about the change from my previous life as an entrepreneur and my current one as an international University student in the Netherlands.

No schedule. If it turns out I have nothing to say for a month, then nothing. But in case I do, I will write it here.
