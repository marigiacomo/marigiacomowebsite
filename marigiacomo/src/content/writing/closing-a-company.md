Delete this text and write the essay. This is the piece only you can write, and the one people
will remember. Some prompts:

- What did the numbers look like right before you knew, and what did you miss in them?
- Which decision, made in year one, made year four inevitable?
- What did you tell the team, and what do you wish you had told them?
- What would you do differently, specifically, not in the abstract?

Be honest and specific. Vague lessons read as evasion; concrete ones read as judgement.
